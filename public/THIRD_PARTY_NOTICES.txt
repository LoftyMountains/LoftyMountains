## OSIRIS

This economic intelligence console follows the map/layer/feed interaction model of [OSIRIS](https://github.com/simplifaisoul/osiris). The chart-based global market adapter is adapted from its markets API. Other application code is independently implemented for Jingxing’s separate Express server and React client.
